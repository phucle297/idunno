Use in idunno (T44 A2): the hooded body/hood mesh and 41-bone rig are the base of the
critter hoodie. Import skips the cape and weapon BoneAttachments and all 76 clips
(`Rogue_Hooded.glb.import`). The game drives the rig from its own procedural clips
(`game/player/toy_character_visual.gd` `sync_rig`). Face, ears, tail, belly, paws and
feet are generated by `tools/asset_generation/critter_parts.gd`.

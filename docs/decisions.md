## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.4a: intensity-paced survival pacing curve

- The director schedules from an explicit pacing curve instead of fixed gaps: `get_pacing()` keeps the forgiving 4s/8s recovery/overlap baseline through the first five minutes, then shortens gaps at intensity 3 (0.75x) and 4+ (0.5x). An earlier minimum-gap floor broke the director fixture's legitimate zero-delay contract (`recovery_duration = 0.0` means schedule immediately), so explicit configuration stays authoritative and only the intensity scales are bounded — the same lesson as other fixture-facing knobs: the game's defaults carry the guarantee, not a clamp on overrides.

## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.2c: Fire + Earthquake burning collapse debris

- Evaluation verdict: keep. The interaction is one shared hazard query, not disaster-to-disaster coupling: `game/main.gd` pushes burning fire-zone positions into `earthquake.set_burning_positions` on `fire.zone_ignited`, `fire.finished` and `earthquake.warning_started` (re-push at quake start keeps it correct after quake `cleanup` clears the set), and Earthquake holds no Fire reference. The HUD shows `FIRE + EARTHQUAKE - BURNING RUBBLE IS FALLING` with both constituent chips suppressed, matching the other composition lines.
- Burning rubble lives in the quake owner beside the collapse: when a section breaks, its quake rubble ignites if it landed within 6.0m of a burning zone — measured horizontally because fire zones are ground areas and a 3D distance hid elevated pieces behind their own height (Toy Town's bridges/gangways sit at the exact-5m 3D boundary). Ignition tags the body (`burning_rubble` group + meta), ember-tints its mesh with a hot core cue and tumbles it out of the flame cluster so the cue reads apart from the flames. No damage tuning here: 20 HP at most once per player at ≥4 m/s within 1m, 6s lifetime, server-side only through `apply_damage` with the explicit `Burning Rubble` cause.
- The debris is short-lived but outlives the quake's active window: `_finish` now resets phase state only, while full `cleanup()` (match reset, director cleanup) clears rubble and burning positions. Clients receive rubble positions in the presentation snapshot as visuals-only markers and hold no bodies; client ticks stay inert. This mirrors meteor's impact-debris contract with its match-reset asymmetry made explicit.
- The composition gate is deterministic without map special-casing: the fixture probes fixed seeds until a seeded collapse lands inside a fire zone, ignites the nearest zone through the real fire flow, and replays the same seed so the identical collapse ignites again (4.2a's same-seed replay property). Lethal asserts stay last; distant players stay safe; the 32-body budget counts the rubble as quake debris.
- Evidence: `EARTHQUAKE_OK checks=88` (ignite/tag/cue, slow-no-damage, bounded once-per-player damage, distant-player safety, client visuals-only snapshot round-trip, rubble outliving the quake then expiring at 6s, five-rematch ignition+cleanup), `DISASTER_COMBINATIONS_OK checks=82` headless / `checks=85` rendered with inspected `compositions-fire-eq-burning-rubble-1280x720.png` capture (named-event wiring, HUD line, seeded ignition, clear `Burning Rubble` death cause, 32-body budget, cleanup), and the full gate `UI_REGRESSION_OK suites=40 matrices=5 persistence_restart=passed` + `DEDICATED_NETWORK_OK processes=3 clients=2 movement=passed rematches=5 recovery=passed`. ## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.2b: Tornado + Meteor bounded tagged impact debris

- Wind-armed impact debris lives in the meteor owner, mirroring fire's `wind_active`/burning-debris pattern: named tornado events (`activated`/`finished`, wired in main beside fire's handlers) call `meteor_shower.set_wind_active`, and a wind-armed impact ejects exactly three tagged bodies (`grabbable` + `meteor_impact_debris` + meta) with the existing capped radial ejection impulse. Solo Meteor is unchanged — impacts stay debris-free without active wind, asserted as a control — so early solo rounds keep their measured behavior.
- Tornado needs no meteor-specific code: debris joins the shared `grabbable` group and the existing bounded wind force loop picks it up, per the checklist's named-events/shared-queries rule. Bounds: three spawned per impact, six alive maximum (oldest freed), 10s lifetime, ejection impulse capped by `prop_impulse_max`, and hits damage at most once per debris per player at ≥5 m/s within 1m — 25 HP plus bounded knockback, server-side only via `apply_damage` with the explicit `Impact Debris` cause. Slow debris never damages.
- Clients render debris as presentation-only visuals at replicated positions (fire's debris pattern generalized to the bounded array); `apply_presentation_snapshot` creates no physics bodies and client ticks never run debris logic, asserted against a client-role ENet instance. Positions ride the existing meteor snapshot so late joiners see them, and the IDLE snapshot path now syncs debris instead of wiping it — debris outlives the strike sequence, which is the window where winds carry it.

Gates now move survivors clear of control impacts, restart between composition gates through the real `prepare_lobby`/`set_player_ready`/`start_match` flow (whose `state_changed` also clears the results overlay), teleport overlapping bodies to distinct carry slots to avoid physics penetration explosions, and keep lethal asserts last.

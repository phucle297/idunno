## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.1a: seeded selection and replayable history

- `start_directing(seed)` randomizes at `seed < 0` or reseeds deterministically at `seed >= 0`, and captures the effective seed (the RNG's readback when auto-seeded). `get_replay_record()` returns `{seed, selection_history}` while a round runs and keeps the finished round's record across `cleanup()` and rematch resets; one structured `DISASTER_REPLAY seed=... history=[...]` line per finished match (dedicated server and local) logs the same data for failure replay.
- Recent-history exclusion was adopted only after measuring repetition as the checklist requires: a scratch probe (5 seeds × 80 selections, deleted after measuring) showed uniform picking produces 29 distance-2 repeats (36%). `RECENT_HISTORY_EXCLUSION = 2` now prefers candidates outside the last two selections and falls back to the full eligible pool when all are recent, so schedules never stall and never become a fixed script.
- `tests/test_disaster_director.gd` grows to 115 checks: same-seed history equality and different-seed divergence through a fresh-manager `_schedule` helper, replay-record retention, no adjacent or distance-2 repeats when eligible sets allow, a 12-selection two-disaster fallback schedule (alternating, never stalling), and five rematch cycles that clear live history while retaining each round's own seed/history record.
- Evidence: `DISASTER_DIRECTOR_OK checks=115`, then supported gate `UI_REGRESSION_OK suites=40 matrices=5 persistence_restart=passed`.

## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.3c: server-selected map identity

- Map identity travels in every playable snapshot (`snapshot.map_id`), and `_apply_match_snapshot` loads a differing map before applying the match state it belongs to, so every client converges on the server's map before the countdown — including clients that join mid-round. Applying only on difference keeps same-identity snapshots from rebuilding the sandbox; `tests/test_map_identity.gd` asserts that determinism.
- `request_map_change` is the single lobby-boundary rule: valid id, actually different, match in LOBBY. Local play and player-hosts apply `set_map_id` directly; owner clients on dedicated rooms route through the existing `_send_owner_action("map:<id>")` owner-action channel, which `_execute_owner_action` validates (owner identity plus session revision) before switching. Once the countdown runs, requests are refused for the round.
- `set_map_id` now reseats players on the new map's spawn anchors via `_reseat_players_for_map` (also used by `return_to_lobby`, replacing its inline copy). Players who joined under the previous map otherwise start embedded in the new geometry — the dedicated test's guest landed inside the new CraneRamp until reseating was added.
- The lobby panel gains a MAP selector above Close: enabled for the session controller at the lobby boundary, read-only reflection of the server selection for everyone else. The panel stack was retightened (the roster keeps its full four-row height that `test_lobby_roster` enforces; 6–8px row gaps and 8px bottom padding) so the panel fits 720p with no overlap or clipping; captures inspected in `.amp/in/artifacts/phase-4/lobby-map/`.

## 2026-10-10 — Task 4.3b: Toy Harbor with map-owned anchors

- Maps now own their gameplay anchors. `game/main.gd` keeps `MAP_IDS`, `map_id`/`map_anchors`, and each `_build_toy_town`/`_build_toy_harbor` returns its own `spawn_origin`, `cover_volumes`, `tornado_paths`, `strike_half_extent`, `fire_zones`+neighbors, `elevation_routes`, `refuges` (two exits each) and `spawn_groups`. `_apply_map_anchors` wires Tornado/Meteor/Lightning/Fire from those anchors and stores them in the `$Sandbox` meta; the hazards gained `set_path_presets`/`set_cover_volumes`/`set_strike_area`/`set_zone_layout` replace-semantics setters, keeping their previous hardcoded layouts only as defaults. Director and hazards no longer hardcode map geometry.
- `set_map_id` rebuilds through `_reset_sandbox()`. It previously called `_build_sandbox()` without freeing the old children, so the two-map gate ran on stacked maps (the Harbor cargo platform overlapped the Toy Town shop center, producing ejections and phantom collision stalls). This is the same reset path rematches use, so map switching and rematch share one ownership rule.
- Toy Harbor geometry follows two walkability rules proven by the gate: ramp tops meet deck/dock faces flush (a top edge buried under the platform blocks the walker at the step-up seam), and dock tops sit at 3.75m, above the 3.5m flood peak, so the docks are honest elevation refuges instead of flood traps. The map carries a two-exit warehouse with breakable roof panel, crane deck on pillars with ramp and edge drop, docks joined by a breakable two-piece gangway, cargo platforms with breakable lids, and four ramps.
- `tests/test_map_variety.gd` is the two-map gate: map/anchor API and hazard wiring, a 20-spawn geometry probe (moving players/demo actors are excluded; static geometry and props still block), four walk-only elevation routes with real arrival asserts, two walked exits per refuge, flood escape per spawn group, no-universal-safe anchor checks, gameplay/overview captures per map, and five rematches restoring breakables, props and map identity. The flood escape contract is "alive with health held through the peak", asserted against the pre-flood value; an absolute 100 assert could be poisoned by damage from an earlier group. GDScript reminder: `:=` cannot infer through untyped node references — the health sample needs an explicit `float`.
- Evidence: `MAP_VARIETY_OK checks=303` headless and `checks=307` rendered with inspected `map-variety-toy_{town,harbor}{Gameplay,Overview}` captures (Toy Town unchanged; Toy Harbor reads grounded, with the low gameplay camera making the gangway seam ambiguous — cosmetic, all connections walk-proven), plus `MAP_SAFETY_OK checks=251` regression.
